# EU261 compensation summary

Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 ("EU261") gives air passengers a right to fixed compensation when a flight is
cancelled, delayed for a long time, or when they are denied boarding against their will.

## Scope

EU261 applies to a flight that departs from an airport in the EU, or that arrives in the EU when it is
operated by an EU carrier. A flight from a non-EU airport into the EU on a non-EU carrier is outside EU261.
The passenger must hold a confirmed booking and have checked in on time.

## Compensation amounts

Compensation depends on the great-circle distance of the flight:

- Up to 1500 km: EUR 250.
- Over 1500 km within the EU, and any other flight between 1500 km and 3500 km: EUR 400.
- Any other flight over 3500 km: EUR 600.

The amount is per passenger, not per booking.

## Reduction for re-routing

If the airline re-routes the passenger and the new flight reaches the final destination within 2 hours
(flights up to 1500 km), 3 hours (1500 to 3500 km) or 4 hours (over 3500 km) of the original arrival time,
compensation is reduced by 50%. This reduction applies to cancellations and denied boarding, not to delays.
